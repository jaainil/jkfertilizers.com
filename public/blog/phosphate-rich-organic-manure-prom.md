## What Is Phosphate Rich Organic Manure (PROM)?

Phosphate Rich Organic Manure (PROM) is an FCO-certified organic bio-phosphatic fertilizer produced by co-composting high-grade micro-pulverized rock phosphate [Ca₅(PO₄)₃(OH,F)] with carbonaceous organic matter and phosphate-solubilizing microorganisms. Delivering a minimum of 10.4% Total P₂O₅ and 7.9% Organic Carbon with a C:N ratio below 20:1, PROM prevents phosphorus fixation in alkaline and calcareous soils through organic acid chelation, providing steady orthophosphate nutrition across the entire crop lifecycle.

## Why Conventional Chemical Phosphorus Fails in High-pH Soils

Phosphorus (P) is vital for ATP energy transport, early adventitious root development, and reproductive flowering. However, when synthetic water-soluble phosphatic fertilizers (such as Di-Ammonium Phosphate or Single Super Phosphate) are broadcast in alkaline soils (pH 7.2 to 8.5):

```
Precipitation Reaction in Alkaline Soils:
Ca(H₂PO₄)₂ (Soluble Phosphate) + 2 Ca²⁺ + 2 OH⁻  →  Ca₃(PO₄)₂↓ (Insoluble Tricalcium Phosphate Precipitate) + 2 H₂O
```

Within 10 to 14 days after application, up to 75% to 80% of soluble chemical phosphorus precipitates into insoluble dicalcium and tricalcium phosphate minerals. Consequently, crops utilize only 15% to 25% of the applied chemical phosphorus during the growing season.

## The Biochemical Solubilization Mechanism of PROM

PROM eliminates phosphorus fixation through microbial bioconversion and organic acid chelation:

### 1. Organic Acid Dissolution Kinetics
During thermophilic co-composting, heterotrophic bacteria and fungi produce aliphatic organic acids (citric, oxalic, malic, and gluconic acids). The carboxyl (-COOH) and hydroxyl (-OH) groups of these organic acids strongly chelate free Calcium cations (Ca²⁺), forming soluble organo-calcium chelates and liberating plant-available monovalent orthophosphate (H₂PO₄⁻).

```
Microbial Chelation Reaction:
Rock Phosphate [Ca₅(PO₄)₃(OH,F)] + Citric Acid (C₆H₈O₇)
  → Calcium Citrate Chelate [Ca₃(C₆H₅O₇)₂] + Bioavailable Orthophosphate (H₂PO₄⁻)
```

### 2. Humic & Fulvic Acid Protection
Humic and fulvic substances in PROM coat the liberated phosphate anions with negative electrical charges, repelling soil clay particles and preventing fixation by active iron and aluminum oxides in acidic soils or calcium in alkaline soils.

### 3. Sustained Root Zone Release
Unlike synthetic DAP, which causes an osmotic salt spike followed by sharp nutrient depletion, PROM releases orthophosphate continuously as soil microbes mineralize the organic carbon matrix over 60 to 90 days.

| **Regulatory Category** | FCO Schedule I (Chemical) | FCO Schedule I (Chemical) | FCO 1985 Schedule IV (Organic Fertilizer) |

## Mandatory FCO 1985 Specifications for PROM

Genuine Phosphate Rich Organic Manure must comply strictly with Indian Fertilizer Control Order (FCO 1985) laboratory testing parameters:

| Quality Parameter | FCO 1985 Statutory Limit | J K Fertilizers Benchmark |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Total Phosphates (as P₂O₅)** | Minimum 10.4% by weight | 10.5% – 12.0% |
| **Organic Carbon** | Minimum 7.9% by weight | 10.0% – 14.0% |
| **Carbon to Nitrogen Ratio (C:N)** | Less than 20:1 | 14:1 – 17:1 |
| **Moisture Content** | Maximum 25.0% by weight | 15.0% – 18.0% |
| **pH (1:5 Solution)** | Not more than 6.7 | 6.5 – 6.7 (Neutral buffered) |
| **Conductivity (1:5 Solution)** | Less than 8.2 dS/m | Below 4.5 dS/m (Seedling safe) |
| **Particle Size** | Min. 90% passing 4.0 mm sieve | Uniform 2–4 mm round granules |

## Recommended Crop Dosage & Application Timing

- **Field Crops (Wheat, Paddy, Maize, Bajra):** 100 kg to 125 kg per acre applied 100% as a basal dressing during seedbed preparation or drilled at sowing.
- **Cash Crops (Cotton, Sugarcane, Potato, Groundnut):** 150 kg to 200 kg per acre split between basal soil preparation (60%) and first vegetative earthing-up (40%).
- **Horticultural Crops & Fruit Orchards (Citrus, Banana, Pomegranate, Mango):** 250 g to 500 g per plant applied in the active drip line root zone prior to seasonal vegetative flush.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can PROM replace DAP on a 1:1 bag basis?
While DAP contains 46% chemical P₂O₅ on paper, up to 75% is locked in alkaline soils (effective uptake = 7 to 10 kg P₂O₅ per bag). PROM delivers 45% to 65% phosphorus use efficiency due to organic chelation. Applying 2 bags of PROM (100 kg) per acre replaces 1 bag of DAP (50 kg) while providing 12 kg of bioavailable carbon and secondary minerals.

### Does PROM burn seedling root hairs in dry soil conditions?
No. Synthetic chemical fertilizers have a high salt index (DAP salt index = 34.2), which draws moisture away from delicate roots via osmotic shock. PROM has a negligible salt index and neutral pH, making it completely safe for germinating seedlings.
