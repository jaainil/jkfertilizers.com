## 1. Engagement & Pricing Models

J K Fertilizers operates on a business-to-business (B2B) model supplying fertilizer brands, agricultural distributors, institutional buyers, and international importers. Pricing varies by volume, formulation customization, packaging specifications, and destination.

### A. Bulk Granule Supply (Direct B2B Purchase)
- **Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ):** 10 Metric Tons (Domestic) / 1 x 20ft FCL (~20–25 MT for Export)
- **Pricing Basis:** Ex-Factory (Vasad, Gujarat), FOR Destination, or FOB/CIF Indian Ports (Mundra / Kandla / Nhava Sheva)
- **Payment Terms:** Standard B2B commercial terms, Letter of Credit (L/C), or Advance / Escrow for export orders.
- **Samples:** Available upon request for qualified B2B buyers and testing laboratories.

### B. Custom Formulation & Contract Manufacturing (Job-Work)
- **Description:** Toll manufacturing and bespoke formulation development to client specifications.
- **Capacity:** High-volume automated granulation and coating lines operating 24/7.
- **Options:** Base granule manufacturing, proprietary biological coating, micronutrient fortification, private recipe formulation.
- **Contract Tiers:** Spot batch runs, seasonal contract manufacturing, and multi-year supply agreements.

### C. Infrastructure & Plant Capacity Leasing
- **Description:** Dedicated production capacity allocation for partner fertilizer companies.
- **Benefits:** Rapid market entry, zero capex for brand owners, full laboratory & QA access.
