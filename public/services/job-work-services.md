# Job Work Services

> **Provider:** J K Fertilizers | **Facility:** Anand, Gujarat, India



J K Fertilizers provides contract manufacturing, toll granulation, and private-label packaging for fertilizer brands, agribusiness companies, and state distribution agencies. We convert raw mineral inputs and organic compounds into uniform granules according to your technical formulations.

Every production run is tracked with in-process laboratory testing for moisture, crush strength, particle size distribution, and active nutrient composition.

## Job Work Capabilities

- **Rotary Drum Granulation**: Conversion of powder ingredients into uniform 2–4 mm spherical granules.
- **Custom Mineral & Bio Formulation**: Blending client-supplied or locally sourced raw minerals with proprietary bio-inoculants and organic matter.
- **Coating & Impregnation**: Applying active microbial cultures, humic extracts, or micronutrients onto base carrier substrates.
- **Crushing, Grinding & Screening**: High-capacity pulverization and multi-deck screening for consistent particle grading.
- **Private Label Bagging**: Packing directly into branded bags, sealing, palletizing, and loading directly onto dispatch trucks.

## Quality Standards

- **In-House Testing**: Routine testing for moisture content, hardness, bulk density, and nutrient concentrations.
- **Batch Documentation**: Full traceability records and certificates of analysis (COA) provided with every dispatched lot.
- **Confidentiality**: Strict non-disclosure protection for proprietary customer recipes and formulations.
