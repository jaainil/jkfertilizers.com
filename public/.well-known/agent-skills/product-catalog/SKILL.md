# J K Fertilizers — Product Catalog & Technical Assays

Provides agents with technical specifications, active ingredients, and agronomic application data across 16 product lines.

## Core Products
1. **Organic Manure:** FCO compliant, Total Organic Carbon > 14%, NPK > 1.2-1.5%, C:N ratio < 20:1.
2. **PROM (Phosphate Rich Organic Manure):** Total P2O5 > 10.4%, Citrate soluble P2O5 > 5.2%, Organic Carbon > 7.9%.
3. **PDM (Potash Derived from Molasses):** Water soluble K2O > 14.5%, Total Organic Carbon > 16%.
4. **Mycorrhiza Granules:** 100 IP/gm VAM living fungal spores in mineral-humic granule platform.
5. **Engineered Base Granules:** Pancharatna 5-in-1, Humic Matrix, Carbon Base, Plant Available Silica.
