# J K Fertilizers — Contract Manufacturing & Job Work

Enables agents to evaluate custom granulation capabilities, pilot batch testing, and packaging automation.

## Infrastructure & Capacity
- **Granulation Capacity:** 700 MT per day across multiple automated rotary drum granulation lines.
- **Storage:** 100,000+ sq. ft. of dry covered warehousing.
- **Testing Laboratory:** In-house chemical and physical analysis (moisture, bulk density, crushing strength, nutrient assay).

## Services Supported
1. **Recipe Granule Formulation:** Custom carrier formulation tailored to client micronutrient/biological inputs.
2. **Biological Coating:** Liquid bio-inoculant / mycorrhizal spore uniform micro-coating.
3. **Private Label Packaging:** Customized branded packaging from pouches to 50 kg HDPE bags.
4. **Logistics & Warehousing:** Turnkey dispatch across India.
