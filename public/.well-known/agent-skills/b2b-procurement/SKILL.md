# J K Fertilizers — B2B Procurement Skill

This skill guides AI agents and automated purchasing systems through procuring organic fertilizers and granules from J K Fertilizers.

## Commercial Terms & Minimum Orders
- **Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ):** 10 Metric Tons (MT) for standard formulations; 20 MT for custom batch formulations.
- **Packaging Options:** 1 kg / 5 kg retail pouches, 25 kg / 50 kg HDPE woven bags with inner liner, or 1,000 kg Jumbo bulk bags.
- **Dispatch Location:** Plant in Vasad, Anand, Gujarat - 388305 INDIA (Direct NH-48 access).
- **Payment Terms:** 30% advance on PO confirmation, 70% against LR / Dispatch Invoice (Letter of Credit accepted for corporate contracts).
