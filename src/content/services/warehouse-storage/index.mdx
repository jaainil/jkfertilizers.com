J K Fertilizers provides covered storage, godown facilities, and dispatch management for fertilizer raw materials and finished goods. Located adjacent to NH-48 in Vasad, Anand, Gujarat, our facility handles bulk truck arrivals and dispatches across Western and Central India.

Our storage units are engineered with moisture barriers, elevated plinths, and active ventilation to prevent caking in moisture-sensitive fertilizers during monsoon seasons.

## Storage Infrastructure

- **Moisture-Protected Warehouses**: Concrete plinths with polyethylene vapor barriers prevent groundwater seepage into stored bags.
- **Segregated Storage Bays**: Separate bays for organic manures, chemical inputs, biological products, and raw minerals to eliminate cross-contamination.
- **Inventory Tracking**: Lot-coded pallet tracking with digital stock ledgers and dispatch documentation.
- **Material Handling**: Forklifts, motorized bag conveyors, and multiple loading docks supporting simultaneous 16-wheel truck loading.

## Safety & Compliance

- **24/7 Security**: Dedicated perimeter security, night illumination, and continuous CCTV recording across all bays and gates.
- **Weighbridge Operations**: 100 MT electronic weighbridge with automated weight slip generation for all inbound and outbound vehicles.
- **Flexible Storage Contracts**: Short-term seasonal buffer storage and multi-year dedicated godown leases available.
