Our granule technology team applies liquid biologicals, humic extracts, and micronutrient coatings onto solid mineral bases. We replace traditional roasted bentonites with clean mineral carriers such as gypsum, silica, and dolomite to deliver both active coatings and secondary soil nutrition.

Controlled rotary drum coating and low-heat air drying ensure high biological spore viability and consistent coating weight across all batches.

## Coating Capabilities

- **Biological Inoculants**: Precise adhesion of mycorrhizal spores, Azotobacter, Rhizobium, and phosphate-solubilizing bacteria (PSB).
- **Humic & Fulvic Fractions**: Uniform surface coating of concentrated potassium humates and fulvic acids.
- **Secondary & Micronutrients**: Targeted application of zinc, boron, sulfur, manganese, and iron complexes.
- **Controlled Moisture Processing**: Low-temperature drying systems protect active living microbes from thermal death.
- **Anti-Caking Additions**: Natural conditioning coatings prevent moisture absorption and caking during seasonal warehouse storage.

## Key Advantages

- **Controlled Release Kinetics**: Coating layers regulate the dissolution rate of active compounds in field soils.
- **Clean Mineral Carrier**: Formulated on low heavy metal substrates (under 10%) compared to raw industrial clays.
- **High Spore Retention**: Proprietary binders keep biological organisms attached to granules during transport and mechanical spreading.
- **Dust Minimization**: Smooth surface conditioning reduces airborne dust in handling facilities.
