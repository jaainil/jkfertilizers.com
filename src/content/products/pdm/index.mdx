description: |
  Potash Derived from Molasses (PDM) is an FCO-certified natural bio-potassium fertilizer manufactured through the controlled condensation and granulation of sugarcane molasses distillery spent wash. It supplies fully water-soluble organic potassium (minimum 14.5% K2O) complexed with plant-derived fulvic substances, bio-organic carbon (min. 16.0%), sulfur, calcium, and trace minerals.

  Unlike synthetic Muriate of Potash (KCl, containing 47% harsh chloride), PDM delivers chloride-safe potassium that avoids root osmotic stress. Potassium cations (K+) stimulate over 60 plant enzymes, including starch synthase and ATPase, accelerating phloem carbohydrate translocation to developing fruits, enhancing grain test weight, improving fruit sugar content (Brix), and prolonging post-harvest storage vitality.
