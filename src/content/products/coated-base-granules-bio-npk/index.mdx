description: |
  Coated Base Granules (BIO NPK) merge a robust mineral core (Calcium, Magnesium, Sulfur, or Silica) with an outer micro-encapsulated layer of biological inoculants comprising Nitrogen-fixing (*Azotobacter / Rhizobium*), Phosphate-solubilizing (*Bacillus megaterium*), and Potassium-mobilizing (*Frateuria aurantia*) bacterial strains.

  Upon soil moisture contact, the outer bio-layer activates to colonize the rhizosphere, enzymatically reducing atmospheric N₂ into plant-available NH₄⁺, excreting organic acids to liberate fixed mineral orthophosphates, and solubilizing clay-bound K⁺ ions. Simultaneously, the slow-disintegrating mineral core delivers secondary calcium (Ca²⁺) and sulfate (SO₄²⁻) to support structural root strength and amino acid synthesis without fertilizer burn.
