description: |
  Base Granules are precision-engineered mineral carrier substrates manufactured from select natural gypsum (CaSO₄·2H₂O), dolomite (CaMg(CO₃)₂), and calcitic minerals. Unlike traditional bentonite clay granules that swell into sticky, impermeable mud upon wetting, our mineral matrix disintegrates smoothly into the soil profile.

  These granules serve a vital dual function: they provide an exceptionally hard, uniform 2–4 mm spherical core that absorbs and holds liquid bio-inoculants, humic extracts, or micronutrient coatings without friability or dust generation, while actively releasing secondary plant nutrients (Calcium, Magnesium, and Sulfate-Sulfur) to condition compacted soils and buffer rhizosphere pH.
