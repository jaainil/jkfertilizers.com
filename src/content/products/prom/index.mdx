description: |
  Phosphate Rich Organic Manure (PROM) is an FCO-certified bio-phosphatic fertilizer produced by co-composting micro-pulverized high-grade rock phosphate (Apatite Ca₅(PO₄)₃(OH,F)) with organic bio-mass, humic substances, and phosphate-solubilizing microorganisms (PSB).

  In alkaline and calcareous soils (pH > 7.2), synthetic water-soluble phosphatic fertilizers like Di-Ammonium Phosphate (DAP) and Single Super Phosphate (SSP) rapidly precipitate with native calcium ions into insoluble tricalcium phosphate, locking up over 70% of applied phosphorus. PROM utilizes low-molecular-weight organic acids (citric, oxalic, and malic acids) generated during composting to continuously chelate calcium cations, maintaining active phosphorus in plant-available orthophosphate forms throughout the 60–90 day crop cycle.
