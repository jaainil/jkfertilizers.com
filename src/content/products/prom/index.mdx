description: |
  Phosphate Rich Organic Manure (PROM) is an FCO-certified bio-phosphatic fertilizer produced by co-composting fine high-grade rock phosphate with organic matter and phosphate-solubilizing microorganisms (PSB).

  In alkaline and calcareous soils, synthetic water-soluble phosphatic fertilizers (DAP/SSP) quickly lock up into insoluble calcium phosphate. PROM provides organic acids from compost that continuously solubilize phosphorus, keeping it plant-available throughout the crop cycle without causing soil acidification or salinity buildup.
