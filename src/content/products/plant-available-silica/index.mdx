description: |
  Plant Available Silica base granules are engineered to deliver bio-absorbable silicon in the biologically active monomeric form (monosilicic/orthosilicic acid, H₄SiO₄). While soil minerals contain abundant silicon, over 99% is immobilized within crystalline silicate minerals unavailable to root uptake.

  Upon application, these granules steadily release H₄SiO₄ molecules into rhizosphere pore water. Roots absorb monomeric silica through specialized nodulin-like channel proteins (Lsi1), translocating it to shoots where it deposits in the cell wall pectin-cellulose network, inducing mechanical stiffness, reducing heavy metal (aluminum/arsenic) toxicity via apoplastic binding, and boosting leaf erectness for superior photosynthetic radiation capture.
