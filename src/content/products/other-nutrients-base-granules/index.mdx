description: |
  Other Nutrients Base Granules are engineered carrier platforms designed to resolve the primary challenge of dry fertilizer blending: ballistic and density segregation of essential micronutrients. By incorporating Zinc (Zn²⁺), Boron (BO₃³⁻), Iron (Fe²⁺/Fe³⁺), Manganese (Mn²⁺), and Sulfate-Sulfur (SO₄²⁻) directly into a homogeneous mineral core, each particle delivers a calibrated micronutrient dose.

  Zinc acts as an essential cofactor for carbonic anhydrase and tryptophan (auxin precursor) synthesis; Boron stabilizes cell wall rhamnogalacturonan-II pectin complexes and pollen tube germination; Iron and Manganese drive photosynthetic electron transport in Photosystem II. Encapsulated in a slow-dissolving mineral carrier, these metallic ions are protected against rapid soil fixation in high-pH calcareous soils.
