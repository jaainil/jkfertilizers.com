  Enriched Base Granules are manufactured by co-granulating natural calcium sulfate (Gypsum CaSO₄·2H₂O), magnesium carbonate (Dolomite CaMg(CO₃)₂), and active trace minerals into a homogeneous mineral core. Unlike standard carriers where nutrients are merely surface-sprayed, our fortification incorporates secondary and micro-elements throughout the particle volume.

The steady, controlled breakdown prevents toxic osmotic spikes while ensuring multi-element availability across 60–75 days.
