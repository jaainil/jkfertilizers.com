  Coated Base Granules (Mycorrhiza) combine a dense, non-friable mineral core (Gypsum, Dolomite, Calcite, or Silica) with a protective outer coating of viable endo-mycorrhizal fungal spores (*Glomus intraradices / Rhizophagus irregularis*).
