  Organic Carbon Base Granules provide a stabilized biological carbon reservoir integrated within a slow-disintegrating mineral skeleton. In agricultural soils where conventional intensive cultivation has depleted Soil Organic Carbon (SOC) below critical 0.5% thresholds, these granules reintroduce aromatic and aliphatic carbon matrices that nourish native soil microbes.

Furthermore, the porous micro-carbon structure functions as an exceptional carrier matrix for custom bio-fertilizer, amino acid, and humic liquid coatings, preventing active agent runoff or volatilization.
