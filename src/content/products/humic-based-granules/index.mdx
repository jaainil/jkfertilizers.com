description: |
  Humic Based Granules combine high-purity Potassium Humate and Fulvic acid biopolymers with a slow-breakdown mineral core. The humic matrix features high densities of active carboxyl (-COOH) and phenolic hydroxyl (-OH) functional groups that deliver an exceptional Cation Exchange Capacity (CEC > 300 meq/100g on organic fraction).

  In alkaline and calcareous soils, these functional groups chemically chelate metallic micronutrient cations (Fe2+, Zn2+, Mn2+, Cu2+), preventing their precipitation into insoluble hydroxides and carbonates. Simultaneously, low-molecular-weight fulvic fractions penetrate root cell membranes, exerting auxin-like metabolic stimulation that accelerates lateral root initiation and ATP synthesis in the rhizosphere.
